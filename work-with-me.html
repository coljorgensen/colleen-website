Hands-On Care

1:1 Osteopathic Treatment

Osteopathy is a whole-body approach to manual therapy that works with the relationships between structure, movement, and function. Sessions are tailored specifically to you, your history, your nervous system, your individual needs and what your body is communicating in that moment.

One-on-one Osteopathy is good for people managing persistent pain, tension, post-injury recovery, pre- and post-surgery, nervous system dysregulation, headaches, post-concussion, eye & jaw concerns, pregnancy, newborns, and much more. Each session includes both hands-on treatment and conversation to help you understand what is happening and why.

  • Chronic and acute pain conditions
  • Post-injury and post-surgical recovery
  • Tension, fatigue, and nervous system support
  • Headaches and jaw (TMJ) concerns
  • Movement and mobility challenges

Aquatic Osteopathy

Booking soon for the 2026 summer season.

Aquatic osteopathy takes place in a residential, outdoor body-temperature pool (92-95 degrees).  Your body is fully supported by Colleen, the water and gentle flotation props.

Water sessions reduce the effects of gravity and allows for effortless movement in all directions. Being in the water feels like coming home to your nervous system and results in a deeper sense of ease. The combination of warmth, buoyancy, support, care and manual techniques makes this approach particularly unique & effective for people living with pain.  Aquatic Osteopathy is deep, meaningful, therapeutic work and an experience like none other.  It is my favourite way to work with people!   

Sessions will be offered from June - September in Dollard des Ormeaux, Qc.

To book an Aquatic Osteopathy session, click the button below.

Unkink your Think

Posture is not just about aesthetics, it's about cognitive function and overall health.  The vessels that supply the brain with oxygenated blood and nutrients and rid the brain of waste pass through the neck.  So neck and head posture are linked to cognitive function and cardiovascular health.

Don't wait until you have an issue.  Be proactive and start correcting your head / neck and upper back posture NOW!
